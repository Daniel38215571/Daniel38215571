## The Thesis
A system is only as good as its ability to survive uncertainty. 

Whether I am architecting a double-entry ledger, forecasting credit risk, or building a 3D game engine, my philosophy remains the same: **Traceability before intelligence.** Explain a number you can already prove, not one you're hoping is right.

## The Progression
My career has been a deliberate progression across three disciplines since 2018:
1. **Accounting (B.Sc. & Deloitte):** Taught me that every number must have a source and a counterparty.
2. **Data Science (M.Sc. & Wizeline):** Taught me how to find patterns in those numbers.
3. **Software Engineering (Primero & Contract):** Taught me how to turn those patterns into systems that run in production.

Today, I operate at the intersection of Data Science, AI Engineering, and Software Engineering, building decision engines for fintech, payments, and logistics.
